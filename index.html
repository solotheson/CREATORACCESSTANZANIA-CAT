How this app works

Use Purchases to add stock with a buying price in TZS and transport. When an item sells, record it in Sales. Use Business position to record capital and see cash, profit and recorded net worth. Use Other Revenue to record extra income that offsets business expenses. Information is saved to your shared database. Purchase cost corrections update profit calculations for existing sales.

CAPITAL & CASH

Business position

All recorded activity from the beginning. Sales, purchases and expenses are paid immediately.

How your money is calculated

Cash = capital invested + loans received + sales + other income − purchases (including transport) − paid expenses − owner withdrawals − loan principal repaid.

Net profit = sales − cost of sold stock + other income − paid expenses. Paying an expense from capital still reduces profit once. Capital investments are not revenue; withdrawals and loan principal are not expenses.

Recorded net worth = cash + remaining stock at cost − outstanding loans. This covers recorded cash, stock and loans only; add any other assets or liabilities separately when assessing your full business value. Cash is combined across cash, bank and mobile money.

Record every capital contribution from the beginning, including capital used to buy your original stock. Do not add today’s cash as another contribution. Existing expenses are treated as already paid—do not enter them again.

PAID EXPENSE

Expense coverage

Identify the source of money already paid. This does not pay the expense again. A source not recorded is not an unpaid bill. Owner capital, other income and borrowing must also exist in your business records; coverage does not create them.

BUSINESS FUNDS

Record funds

Enter money actually received or paid. Record loan interest in Expenses. Record principal here only. To correct an entry, edit it; delete only mistakes, never actual payments.
