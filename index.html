How this app works

Use Purchases to add stock with a buying price in TZS and transport. When an item sells, record it in Sales; stock, profit and cash figures update automatically. Use Other Revenue to record extra income that offsets business expenses. Information is saved to your shared database. Purchase cost corrections update profit calculations for existing sales.
